## 2. Recommended Primary and Alternative Target Venues

### Primary Target: **IEEE Internet of Things Journal (IEEE IoT-J)**
- **Strategic Justification:** IEEE IoT-J is the premier journal for the Social Internet of Things. It published the foundational trust routing paper by Marche et al. [7] and numerous trust-aware service discovery studies. The blend of rigorous mathematical trust modeling (Laplace smoothing + entropy-weighted indirect feedback + Jaccard social proximity) and multi-objective metaheuristic optimization perfectly matches the core readership and editorial scope.
- **Anticipated Reviewer Focus:** 
  1. Realism of the SIoT simulation layer and social relationship modeling.
  2. Concrete physical QoS and energy trade-offs.
  3. Defense resilience against coordinated collusion and on-off attacks.
- **Pre-submission Actions:** Ensure IEEE Transactions double-column template compliance and highlight physical network constraints.

### Alternative Option 1: **Applied Soft Computing (Elsevier)**
- **Strategic Justification:** If the editorial emphasis prioritizes algorithmic metaheuristic innovation, Applied Soft Computing is exceptionally suited for novel hybrid nature-inspired optimizers (combining DBO exploration with MPA exploitation). The journal strongly values comprehensive statistical benchmarking (Wilcoxon, Friedman) and systematic ablation studies.
- **Anticipated Reviewer Focus:** Convergence proofs, parameter sensitivity, and comparison against recent swarm algorithms.

### Alternative Option 2: **IEEE Transactions on Network and Service Management (TNSM)**
- **Strategic Justification:** TNSM focuses on service-oriented routing, autonomic service composition, and network resource optimization. The formal SIoT graph formulation $G=(V, E)$, service request model $R$, and multi-objective Pareto front make it a prime candidate.
